Shop with confidence

Trust and safety are part of the CAMPUS CART experience.

🛡️ Marketplace safety

  • Seller verification and verified seller badges.
  • Admin review of seller applications and new listings.
  • Report seller or listing controls.
  • Reviews intended for buyers with completed orders.
  • Order references and a dispute reporting process.
  • Use public, well-lit campus meet-up points.
  • Keep important order conversations on the platform.

⚠️ Avoid scams

  • Do not share your password or one-time verification code.
  • Do not pay into a personal account outside the official checkout.
  • Check product photos, description and seller information.
  • Inspect items at collection where possible.
  • Report suspicious sellers, payment requests or listings.
  • Never meet alone in an isolated location.
Demo notice: This HTML-only version stores demo data in this browser. Seller verification, student ID checks, secure payments, real messaging, admin access controls and payout protection are not live services. Do not upload real identity documents or enter real payment credentials.

Built for campus enterprise.

CAMPUS CART connects student buyers with student entrepreneurs. It gives sellers a simple storefront to showcase products and services, and gives buyers a convenient place to discover campus businesses.

Listings are free in this demo. A small commission is intended to apply only to completed orders; the final commission rate will be set by CAMPUS CART.

Frequently asked questions

Who can sell?

The planned marketplace is for verified students. This demo lets you submit a seller application for review.

Does it cost to list?

No. Listings are free. A commission is planned only on completed orders.

How do I pay?

The demo checkout offers Mobile Money, card and pay on delivery as selectable examples. No real payment is processed.

How do I report a problem?

Use the report option on a product or seller, or submit a dispute from your orders.
